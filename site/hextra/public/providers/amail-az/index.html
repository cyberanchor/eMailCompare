Notes

Amail is an Azerbaijani email service operating under the .az domain, offering more than just email functionality. It includes additional features such as weather updates and a cloud storage service (Drive) for uploading, storing, and managing files. The service operates under Azerbaijani law, which governs potential government requests. Use Cases: Amail is suitable for basic communication, local use in Azerbaijan, or storing non-critical files in the cloud. It’s convenient for users who want extras like weather updates and Drive in one interface, particularly if they trust Azerbaijan’s jurisdiction. However, due to unclear security and privacy policies, it’s not recommended for confidential correspondence, anonymous use, or handling sensitive data (unlike Proton Mail or Tutanota). It’s best as a simple tool for everyday tasks with moderate protection expectations.
