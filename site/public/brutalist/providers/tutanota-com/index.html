Notes

Tutanota is a secure email service with end-to-end encryption, developed by the German company Tutao GmbH, focused on privacy and security. It’s ideal for anonymous communication, storing contacts and calendars, and protecting data from surveillance. Key features: full data encryption (email, calendar, contacts), no tracking, minimal data collection, and servers in Germany under strict GDPR compliance. Tutanota offers top-tier privacy with end-to-end encryption, ensuring even developers can’t access your data. For anonymity, no phone or external email is required for signup, IP addresses aren’t logged by default, and email headers are stripped of metadata, making it great for concealing your identity.
