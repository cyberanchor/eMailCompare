Domain registered 2026-08-10; the Wayback Machine captures from 2004-03-25 to 2019-05-26 belong to an earlier site on the domain, and there are none from 2026, so online since is left empty. The site ran a crypto swap service before mail (stopped 2026-11-20); older accounts still log in with an email or a 16-digit account number. Addresses on @foldoy.com and @evil.vg (free) and @veil.no (paid). Sign-up asks only for an address and a password (12+ characters), recovery email optional, proof of work instead of a captcha; no phone number, name or ID. Accounts made over a VPN or Tor are locked until a $10 deposit or manual approval; at most 1 new account a day and 3 in 30 days per connection. Free: 1 GB, 1 receive-only alias, 5 emails a day (20 after 14 days), a one-time $5 sending unlock (50 a day); unused free accounts are deleted after 12 months. foldoy+ $4/month or $48/year: 15 GB, 50 aliases, 200 emails a day, 1 custom domain. Polar $7/month or $84/year: 50 GB, 500 aliases, 5 custom domains, 10 extra addresses. Founding-member prices are kept while the subscription stays active. Plans are paid from a US dollar balance topped up with Monero, Bitcoin or Litecoin ($10 to $2,000) into foldoy's own watch-only wallets; card payments through Link (Stripe) are announced but off. Encryption: OpenPGP.js keys made in the browser; the private key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under an Argon2id value of the password that never reaches the server. Mail between foldoy users in the webmail and from PGP senders is end-to-end, subject included; other incoming mail arrives readable and is encrypted with the user's key before it is stored, subject included except in three cases listed on the site; mail to other providers leaves readable. From, to, time, size, delivery and spam headers, settings and filter rules stay readable to the server. Optional privacy+ key that foldoy cannot reset; a recovery code keeps old mail, a recovery-email reset makes a new key. IMAP and SMTP for Thunderbird and other apps on the free plan with app passwords (the key is added to the app by the user), Gmail import, an alias key per alias, remote images fetched through the server, an HTML-only mode without JavaScript. Only the webmail client is published (github.com/FoldoyMail/foldoy-mail, AGPL-3.0, repository created 2026-11-20, organisation 2026-11-18); the server code is not. Mail arrives through a server in Oslo (ServeTheWorld AS); the privacy policy says mailboxes are stored on its own server that is not in Norway yet, without naming the country. Website and webmail behind Cloudflare; system mail sent through Resend. IMAP mx.foldoy.com:993 answers (Stalwart); SMTP 465 and 587 are published in autoconfig but were not reachable from the test network. Mail DNS: SPF ~all, DMARC p=quarantine; no MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, DNSSEC or DANE.
