  Another independent, interactive email service comparison project. Jurisdiction, intelligence alliance,

# The "about" block under the summary cards.
overview: >-
  Compares a wide range of email providers, from privacy-focused services to general-purpose platforms.
  Every value comes from public sources (DNS, RDAP/WHOIS, the providers' own sites and policies) and lives
  in one file, emails.csv.
features: [search, status and country filters, sortable columns, colour hints, domain list export (.txt)]
criteria:
  - [security, "end-to-end encryption, SSL/TLS"]
  - [privacy, "data collection, IP logging, third-party sharing, personal data"]
  - [jurisdiction, "server location and the privacy law that applies"]
  - [alliance, "Five, Nine or Fourteen Eyes membership of that country"]
  - [registration, "email or phone verification at signup"]
  - [pricing, "free tier and premium plans"]
  - [terms, "usage restrictions: spam, hacking, commercial use"]
  - [infrastructure, "A / MX records, hosting network, company owner"]
  - [domain, "primary and other domains, creation and expiry dates"]
