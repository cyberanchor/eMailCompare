      Notes

      Emailn.de is a German email service by Norbert Sehm, offering webmail with a free ad-supported version and likely paid options (though not specified in the provided data). It focuses on standard communication features, integrated with analytics and ad services. Suitable for basic correspondence and everyday tasks. Security & Anonymity: Employs SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission, providing basic connection security, but lacks end-to-end encryption, leaving content vulnerable. Anonymity is low: IP addresses, form data, and cookies are collected for ads (Google AdSense, Pixfuture, Refinery89) and shared with U.S.-based tools (WordPress Stats, Google), where data protection is weaker under EU standards. Data is stored in Germany (NBIServ) but can be disclosed to authorities upon request. Suitable for casual personal use, such as temporary correspondence or site registrations where privacy isn’t critical. Not for confidential purposes—falls short of Proton Mail or Tutanota due to poor anonymity and lack of full content protection. Ads and data sharing limit its appeal for security-conscious users.
