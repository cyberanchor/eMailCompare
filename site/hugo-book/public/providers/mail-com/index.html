      Notes

      Mail.com is an American email service by 1&1 Mail & Media Inc., offering unique email addresses, cloud storage, and basic organizer features. It’s suitable for everyday communication, file storage, and casual use. Security & Anonymity: It uses SSL encryption for data transmission but lacks end-to-end encryption, reducing content privacy. Anonymity is limited: requires personal data (name, DOB) at signup, logs IPs, and shares info with third parties for ads, making it unsuitable for privacy-focused users. Mail.com works well for simple personal or temporary needs but falls short in security and anonymity compared to services like Proton Mail or Tutanota.
