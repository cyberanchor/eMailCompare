      Notes

      Mailo is a European email service by French company Mail Object, offering email, calendar, storage, and chat. It suits personal use, families (with Mailo Junior for kids), and professionals on paid plans. Security & Anonymity: Features SSL encryption and antispam, but lacks end-to-end encryption, limiting security. Anonymity is weak: requires personal data (DOB), logs IPs (1 month), and blocks third-party validation emails on free accounts. Usage: Sign up on mailo.com with name, DOB, and password; access via web, apps, or IMAP/SMTP. Multi-accounts need paid plans and proxies due to automation bans. Decent for basic communication and family features, but lags in anonymity and security compared to Proton Mail or Tutanota.

      Illegal actions (spam, hacking, harassment, IP theft), multiple free accounts for 3rd-party registration, automation, business use on free plan

      Collect email, personal info (name, DOB, optional), IP logs (1 month), payment data, content (encrypted). Store in France, delete after 1 month post-account closure. Share with payment providers (Verifone/Paybox, Mobiyo), Gandi (domains), legal requests only.
